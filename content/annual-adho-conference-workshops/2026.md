2026 marks the 10th iteration of AudioVisual & DH Special Interest Group (AVinDH SIG) events at the ADHO annual conference. Established in 2014, the SIG has now reached the point where it is timely to reflect on the developments in the field, and to jointly plan the future of our community.  

The theme of the DH2026 emphasizes “Engagement.” Building on this idea, the AVinDH SIG invites all ADHO conference attendees to join this workshop to share work in progress and to discuss the present state and future avenues of research on AV materials within the Digital Humanities.
You do not have to be a member of the AVinDH SIG to [register](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1D0mRufNv9C8kqhJB6LxNBiZwk-DT2ImsBA4wX8umXdo/viewform?edit_requested=true), although we would be delighted to have you join our mailing list
[here](https://groups.google.com/u/2/g/avindh?pli=1)!

## Workshop Schedule

Time: Monday, July 27, 2026

### 10:00 -- 10:30 Welcome

Introduction to AVinDH.

### 10:30 -- 11:30 Speed Dating

A structured networking activity.

### 11:30 -- 12:00 Break


### 12:00 -- 12:45 Panel

“AVinDH SIG turns 10! Reflecting on our past, present and future”

*Discussants:* Lauren Tilton (University of Richmond), Michaël Bourgatte (Université de Lorraine), and Yumeng Hou (National University of Singapore).  

*Moderator:* Alison Langmead (University of Pittsburgh)


### 12:45 -- 14:00 Lunch Break

### 14:00 -- 16:00 Lightning Talks

This session will focus on current research on AVinDH. Each participant will have 5 minutes to introduce their work. 

### 16:00 –- 16:15 Break

### 16:15 –- 17:15 Tool Cavalcade 

5-minute presentations about AVinDH tools.

### 17:15 – 17:45 Wrap up
Final reflections on the future of AVinDH.
